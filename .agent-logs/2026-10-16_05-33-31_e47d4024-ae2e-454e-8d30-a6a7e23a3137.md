## Keyboard, screen readers, reduced motion (WCAG 2.2 AA)

- **Focus** is always visible: a 2 px amber ring with a 2 px background offset, on every control, in both themes.
- **Keyboard:** skip link to main; sidebar is a `nav` with the current page marked; tabs use arrow keys; transcript lines are buttons in a `list` (arrow keys move between lines, Enter seeks); the player has space (play/pause), ←/→ (5 s), ↑/↓ (speed), `/` focuses find, `n`/`p` next/previous match, `?` shows the shortcuts dialog. Nothing needs a mouse.
- **Screen readers:** one `h1` per page; landmarks (`header`, `nav`, `main`, `aside` for notes); the player exposes the transcript as a `<track kind="captions">`; the current line is announced via a polite live region at most every 10 s; state changes (bot joined, summary ready, spell sent) use a polite live region; gaming labels carry plain descriptions; the timestamp button reads as "Jump to 12 minutes 34 seconds".
- **Reduced motion:** the landing stage shows its final frame; transcript auto-scroll uses `scroll-behavior: auto`; no spinners that pulse, skeletons fade only; toasts appear without slide.
- **Contrast:** AA for all text and 3:1 for UI borders and the focus ring in both themes; amber never used for small text.
- **Touch:** 44 px targets; the mini player sits above the bottom nav with safe-area padding.
- **Zoom:** 200 % and 375 px with no horizontal scroll; the two-column meeting page stacks.
