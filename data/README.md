# Seed data

AFK.ai's demo meetings are real recorded meetings from the **ICSI Meeting Corpus**
(International Computer Science Institute, Berkeley, 2000–2002), distributed by the
University of Edinburgh under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence](https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/icsi/license.shtml).
Corpus home: https://groups.inf.ed.ac.uk/ami/icsi/

These are natural research-group meetings, not scripted or role-played. Participants are
identified only by anonymous corpus codes (for example `me013`), so the app labels them
"Speaker A", "Speaker B" and so on. First names spoken during a meeting stay in the
transcript exactly as the corpus publishes them; the corpus itself maps no code to a name.

## Changes we made (required disclosure under CC BY 4.0)

- Transcripts were converted from the corpus's `.mrt` XML into `meetings/<id>.json` by
  [`tools/icsi/parse_mrt.py`](../tools/icsi/parse_mrt.py): one entry per speaker turn with
  start and end times. The digit-reading task (a span of a few minutes in each meeting
  but Bed017, not always at the end: Bmr007 at 55:08 to 59:49, Bmr021 at 30:45 to 34:45,
  Bro015 at 12:13 to 16:52 with speech in between) and non-speech annotations (laughter,
  coughs, pauses) were removed from the transcript; the audio was not cut, so while
  those spans play the page shows the last turn that started. Transcriber markup was
  normalised (`I_B_M` → `IBM`, `@@` → `[unclear]`). The committed JSON was made before a
  fix to the converter's non-speech filter, which also dropped a few one-word segments
  spelled from the letters of "unclear" ("a", "can.", "u-", "nnn"); they are missing from
  the transcript and search while the audio says them. Re-running the converter restores
  them but renumbers every turn, which the committed notes cite, so it waits for a
  regeneration of the notes.
- Titles are the research group's name (the corpus has no meeting titles). Dates are the
  corpus's timestamp in Berkeley local time.
- Audio is the corpus's mixed headset channel (`<id>.interaction.wav`), re-encoded to
  AAC for streaming. Nothing was cut or rearranged.
- Summaries, chapters, action items and decisions were generated by AFK.ai with Claude and
  are not part of the corpus.

available from the corpus site.
